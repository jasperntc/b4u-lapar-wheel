# Logos

The mark is a gift tag with a bold condensed "4", hung on a string. The tag is `stamp`; the "4" is `on-stamp`; the string is `ink`. Each file has its colours baked in, because an `<img>` can't inherit them.

- `b4u-mark-light.svg`: the tag on its own, for light and kraft grounds. Use it in stories, video intros and avatars.
- `b4u-mark-dark.svg`: the same tag for dark grounds (light-violet tag, dark "4").
- `b4u-lockup-light.svg` and `b4u-lockup-dark.svg`: the tag plus the "Built4You" wordmark in condensed Bricolage Grotesque. Use them in the site header and the hub footer.
- `b4u-favicon.svg`: the tag without its string, square, switching to the dark colours by itself under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`. Use it as the browser icon for the hub and every episode site. Export a 180px PNG of it for the Apple touch icon.

Rules:
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least half the tag's width.
- Don't recolour, rotate, outline or put the mark on a photo. In video, the tag may swing from the top of its string.
- The wordmark is always one colour (`ink`). Don't pick out the "4".
- The wheel is the first episode's motif, not the series logo. Keep it out of the mark.
